A new category of workforce

Not another copilot.
An operating role.

A DFE is a purpose-built digital worker with a defined mission, operating boundaries, systems access, escalation rules, and measurable outcomes. It is designed to become part of the way your team works.

01 · Role

Named accountability

Each worker has a clear remit, success measures and an owner on the enterprise side. No vague “AI transformation” brief.

02 · Context

Connected to the work

DFEs operate across approved systems, knowledge and workflows—so the output is relevant to your environment, not a generic answer.

03 · Control

Governed by design

Access boundaries, human checkpoints, audit trails and verification are part of the operating model from day one.

The operating loop

From signal
to proof.

Enterprise AI earns trust by making its reasoning, authority and outcomes visible. The loop is designed to be inspected at every stage.

01 / CONNECT

Understand

Map the role, systems, data and operating boundary.

02 / OBSERVE

Observe

Collect grounded signals from approved sources.

03 / DECIDE

Prioritise

Explain impact and recommend the next best action.

04 / GOVERN

Approve

Pause consequential work for human review.

05 / EXECUTE

Act

Perform only the authorised, bounded change.

06 / VERIFY

Prove

Confirm the outcome and retain the evidence.
